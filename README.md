### `> whoami`

**Dawn Gibson Wages** (she/her). Founder of [Wildflower Software](https://wildflowersoftware.com) and [Wildflower Labs](https://labs.wildflowersoftware.com).  
Former PSF Board Chair. Former Director of Community & DevRel @ Anaconda.  
Django engineer turned community builder turned DevRel leader, now building AI agents that hold up in the real world.

I started coding on MySpace in 2005, walked away from finance in 2015, and never looked back.  
These days I build agent systems for clients, teach teams how to ship AI that's correct and not just convincing, and still make the occasional Star Trek demo.

> *"Things need to be tended to to grow — and that's a good metaphor for open source."*

**Currently:**
- 🌼 [Wildflower Software](https://wildflowersoftware.com): Python & Django consulting. Backends, data, and AI features in production
- 🔬 [Wildflower Labs](https://labs.wildflowersoftware.com): workshops, talks and advisory on the final mile of enterprise AI
- 🪐 [Satyrn AI](https://www.satyrn.ai): Jupyter for agents, local-first for Python developers
- 🖖 Writing [*Domain-Driven Django*](https://domaindrivendjango.com)
- 🧭 Open to leadership and board roles, consulting, and speaking
